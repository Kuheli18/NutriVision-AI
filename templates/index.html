04 — ABOUT NUTRIVISION

Technology meets
everyday nutrition.

NutriVision AI is an educational machine learning project designed to explore food recognition and nutritional estimation using image analysis.

It uses pretrained AI models and approximate nutrition reference values. A photograph cannot reveal exact food weight, hidden ingredients, contamination or whether food is safe to eat.
